On craft

MATE
RIALS

Structure & diffusion

Perfume is made from materials.

Not marketing. Not celebrity campaigns. Not packaging.

Each composition begins with raw aroma chemicals and natural extracts.

Cedarwood Ambroxan Hedione Vetiver Iso E Super Labdanum Rose Absolute Benzoin Galaxolide Bergamot

Learning how they interact — how one chemical amplifies another, how concentration shifts character entirely — is the real work of perfumery.

Lucky Obscura is built slowly through experimentation, structure, and curiosity about what scent can become.

Working Notebook

THE
LABORATORY

The place where materials are studied, accords are tested, and compositions evolve. Fragments from the working notebook.

Nov 2024
CEDAR-VETIVER ACCORD — CV-03

Cedarwood Virginia with vetiver bourbon. Iso E Super introduced at 15% — smoky diffusion without aggression. → Foundation for Cedar Dusk.

Accord Development
Dec 2024
MINERAL CITRUS STUDY — MC-01

Bergamot and grapefruit with Calone at trace levels. Unexpected lift when Hedione exceeded 20%. → Direction for Quiet Projection.

Material Study
Jan 2025
DARK FLORAL — NP-02

Rose absolute + patchouli + trace oud. Very dark floral profile. Oud pulls masculine above 3%. Hedione at 8% lifts without sweetening. → Confirmed direction for Noir Pétale.

Development
Mar 2025
ISO E SUPER — CONCENTRATION STUDY

Tested at 5%, 10%, 20% in alcohol on strips. 5%: almost imperceptible. 10%: clean balance, good projection. 20%: overpowers, headache-inducing. → 10% ceiling for Cedar Dusk.

Material Study
May 2025
AMBER STRUCTURE — AS-02

Ambroxan, labdanum, and benzoin tested in three ratios. Version B produced the smoothest drydown. → Possible base for Noir Pétale and Series II.

Base Accord
Ongoing
SERIES II — GYM-BL-03 PROTOTYPE

Too much dihydromyrcenol — metallic, synthetic top. Needs more citrus. Bergamot + grapefruit being tested. Vetiver base is solid. Top needs 2–3 more iterations. → Not ready.
