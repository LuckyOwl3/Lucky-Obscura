Obscura No. 01

CEDAR
DUSK

Cold wood. Iron light. A low amber burning.

45 € Extrait de Parfum · 30ml Add to Order
The composition

MOOD &
CHARACTER

Cedar Dusk opens with a sharp, mineral dryness — Iso E Super at controlled concentration, enough to project without dominating. The cedarwood arrives mid-wear, grounded and slightly smoky through the guaiacwood accord. Vetiver pulls the base into earth and shadow. The amber glow is restrained. It never sweetens. It only deepens.

From the laboratory

DEVELOPMENT
NOTES

CEDAR-VETIVER ACCORD — CV-03

Cedarwood Virginia with vetiver bourbon. Iso E Super introduced at 15% — smoky diffusion without aggression. → Foundation for Cedar Dusk.

Accord Development
ISO E SUPER — CONCENTRATION STUDY

Tested at 5%, 10%, 20% in alcohol on strips. 5%: almost imperceptible. 10%: clean balance, good projection. 20%: overpowers, headache-inducing. → 10% ceiling for Cedar Dusk.

Obscura No. 02

NOIR
PÉTALE

Petals pressed in dark resin. Still. Unresolved.

45 € Extrait de Parfum · 30ml Add to Order
The composition

MOOD &
CHARACTER

Noir Pétale is a floral that refuses to be soft. Hedione opens it with a transparent, jasmine-like lift — then rose absolute arrives, dark and waxy rather than fresh. Geranium adds an unexpected green edge. The base is amber built from labdanum and benzoin: resinous, warm, slow to fade. This is a floral for people who don't usually wear florals.

From the laboratory

DEVELOPMENT
NOTES

DARK FLORAL — NP-02

Rose absolute + patchouli + trace oud. Very dark floral profile. Oud pulls masculine above 3%. Hedione at 8% lifts without sweetening. → Confirmed direction for Noir Pétale.

Development
AMBER STRUCTURE — AS-02

Ambroxan, labdanum, and benzoin tested in three ratios. Version B produced the smoothest drydown. → Possible base for Noir Pétale and Series II.

The Collection LUCKY OBSCURA
Obscura No. 03

QUIET
PROJECTION

Daylight through glass. Something moving in still air.

45 € Extrait de Parfum · 30ml Add to Order
The composition

MOOD &
CHARACTER

Quiet Projection opens bright — bergamot and grapefruit, clean and immediate. Then Hedione takes over: a transparent, weightless quality that diffuses into the air rather than sitting on skin. Iso E Super adds a woody shimmer in the heart. Ambroxan in the base gives it staying power without heaviness. This is the lightest composition in Series I. Effortless to wear. Harder to stop noticing.
